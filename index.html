The plan

Backtest the signal

Runs the exact confluence score shown on the chart against history: enter next open when the score crosses the threshold, exit at an ATR stop, an R-multiple target, or a time stop.
PlutoSwing is decision support, not advice. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell any security, and no signal, score, or backtest can guarantee outcomes — markets are probabilistic and past performance never assures future results. Data may be delayed or simulated (demo mode is clearly flagged). Trade with money you can afford to risk, and size positions so that being wrong is survivable — that, not accuracy, is what keeps traders in the game.

Settings

Everything is held in memory only — nothing is stored or sent anywhere except your data provider.

Data source

Live mode uses Twelve Data — a free key (≈800 credits/day, 8/min) is available at twelvedata.com. One chart load = 1 credit; the app fetches lazily, caches for 10 minutes, and automatically paces bulk loads to the 8-requests/minute free limit. Symbol search covers every listed ticker worldwide — including new IPOs — and costs no credits. If live requests fail inside a chat preview, download this file and open it directly in your browser. Tip: if you keep this file locally, you can paste your key into DEFAULT_API_KEY at the top of the script so it's pre-filled.
